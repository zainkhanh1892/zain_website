AI Systems · RAG · SFT

Agentic AI for AI NIC Cluster Debugging

AI NIC cluster failures generate thousands of log lines with no clear signal. The hard part was deciding what to retrieve — too broad and the LLM hallucinates, too narrow and it misses the cause. I built a retrieval pipeline over telemetry and logs with supervised fine tuning to teach the model what a meaningful failure pattern looks like, then orchestrated tool use to let it reason across multiple sources before surfacing candidates.

Outcome: cut triage from ~4 hours of manual log searching to targeted root-cause candidates in under 10 minutes.

Stack: Python, FastAPI, Qdrant, Docker

AI Enabled SOC Engine for Alert Triage

SOC analysts were spending the first 20 minutes of every alert just reading through Jira history and attachments to build context. The ingestion problem was the trickiest part — Jira attachments, comments, and linked issues all needed different chunking strategies to stay retrievable. I built a project-scoped pipeline that ingests heterogeneous sources and surfaces ranked incident summaries in seconds.

Outcome: scaled ingestion to 2,000+ issues with consistent retrieval latency under 2 seconds.

Security Ops · Agentic AI · MCP

AI SOC Capstone — Automated Alert Triage

Manual SOC alert triage is repetitive and slow — an analyst pulls a Jira ticket, looks up the indicator on VirusTotal, makes a call, and writes it up. I automated that entire loop: GPT-4o orchestrates tool calls to fetch the alert, enrich it, classify the threat, and post a structured verdict back to Jira with no human in the loop. The tricky part was making the LLM verdict trustworthy — sanity checks override GPT when its classification contradicts raw detection counts.

Outcome: end-to-end triage in under 60 seconds with full audit history and a React dashboard for analysts.

Stack: Python, FastAPI, OpenAI, SQLite, React, Vite, Tailwind
